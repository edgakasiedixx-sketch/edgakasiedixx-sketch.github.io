About Me

My background, journey and ambitions.

I was born in Tanzania in 2007. My educational journey began in 2012, when I started primary school. I continued my primary education until 2020 and achieved Grade A in every subject in my national examinations.

I then joined Tabora Boys Secondary School, a school where Tanzania's Father of the Nation, Mwalimu Julius Nyerere, studied. I spent four years there before sitting for the Form Four National Examinations (NECTA CSEE), in which I achieved Division I with 7 points.

In 2025, I joined Rungwe High School in Mbeya City. I am studying Physics, Advanced Mathematics and Computer Science, with my studies planned to run from 2025 to 2027.
